# ClientoraHQ Free Tools: ads

The creatives are in this folder. Each angle comes in three sizes:

| File | Size | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| `pdf-1080x1080.png`, `kit-1080x1080.png`, `privacy-1080x1080.png` | 1080×1080 | Facebook and Instagram feed, LinkedIn feed |
| `pdf-1080x1920.png`, `kit-1080x1920.png`, `privacy-1080x1920.png` | 1080×1920 | Instagram and Facebook Stories and Reels, WhatsApp Status |
| `pdf-1200x627.png`, `kit-1200x627.png`, `privacy-1200x627.png` | 1200×627 | LinkedIn single-image ad, Facebook link ad |

To change the wording, edit `render-ads.mjs` and run `node marketing/ads/render-ads.mjs`. It launches Edge once and rewrites all 9 PNGs.

Every claim below matches the live site: free, nothing is uploaded, no sign-up, no daily limits, and the screen recorder has no time limit. Don't add claims the tools don't back up, such as "faster than X" or "used by 10,000 people".

## Targeting (a starting point)

- **Countries:** Pakistan, USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Europe (same as the app's target markets). Keep India out, matching the app.
- **Facebook and Instagram interests:** Freelancing, Upwork, Fiverr, Small business owners, Graphic design, Web design, Digital marketing. Ages 20–45.
- **LinkedIn:** Job functions Marketing, Business Development, Arts and Design. Company size 1–10 or self-employed. Skills: freelancing, web design, graphic design.
- **Budget:** start with about $5 a day per angle for 5–7 days, then move the money to the angle with the lowest cost per click. These ads drive traffic to free tools; the tool pages then show the ClientoraHQ offer.
- **Free, organic use:** post the same images on your own LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook pages and in WhatsApp Status. The 1080×1920 files fit Status as-is.
